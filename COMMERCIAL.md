# Commercial Licence

Nefo is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3](LICENSE) (AGPLv3). **Every feature is free for everyone, including businesses.** The commercial licence doesn't unlock anything. It gives you different legal terms and a business relationship with the maintainers.

## You do not need a commercial licence if you…

- manage your own servers with Nefo, for any purpose, including commercial ones
- manage your clients' websites with Nefo, as an agency or freelancer, without changing Nefo's code
- offer an **unmodified** Nefo to your hosting customers and point them to this repository for the source code
- change Nefo and publish your changes under the AGPLv3

## You may want a commercial licence if you…

- **change Nefo and offer it to others over a network without publishing your changes.** Section 13 of the AGPLv3 requires you to publish them otherwise.
- **rebrand Nefo** (white-label) for your hosting company or product
- **embed Nefo**, or parts of it, in a proprietary product
- work in an organisation whose legal policy does not allow AGPL software

## What a commercial licence includes

- the right to use, change and distribute Nefo under proprietary terms, including white-labelling
- an invoice and a signed licence agreement for your records and audits
- priority support with an agreed response time (SLA)
- advance notice of security fixes before public disclosure
- help migrating from cPanel, Plesk, DirectAdmin or other panels

Prices depend on the number of servers and the support level. Contact **sales@nefo.io**.

## Why this model

Dual licensing lets us keep Nefo completely free for the community while companies that build products on it fund its development. To keep this possible, contributors sign a [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md).
