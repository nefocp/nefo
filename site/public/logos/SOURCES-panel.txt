# Logo sources

These SVG files identify software and applications in the panel (web servers, PHP, databases, runtimes, the
operating system and app-store applications). Each third-party file was downloaded into its own new, empty
folder and rebuilt by an allow-list sanitizer that keeps only plain shapes and gradients (no scripts, styles,
text, images, links or external references). See the app store's research folder for the raw checksums.

**Licences.** Devicon files are MIT (see `LICENSE-MIT.md`), Simple Icons files are CC0-1.0 unless noted,
Lobe Icons and code-server files are MIT, FileBrowser Quantum is Apache-2.0. The licences cover the SVG files;
the logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify their software (nominative use). Do not
alter the marks beyond resizing.

**Own icons.** OpenLiteSpeed, ModSecurity (the WAF) and ClamAV publish no SVG logo we can use, so
`openlitespeed.svg`, `waf.svg` and `clamav.svg` are simple generic icons drawn for this project. They do not
imitate the projects' marks.

**Dark mode.** Several logos are dark on transparent. The UI always shows logos on a white rounded tile
(`.logo-tile`), in both themes.
